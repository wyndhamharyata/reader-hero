Anne of Windy Poplars
CHAPTER V

A Full-fledged Schoolma'am

That morning Anne turned away from the window with a sigh and went down to breakfast. The day was the first of September, and the school at Avonlea opened on it. She had been dreaming of this hour all summer, and now that it had come she was not at all sure she wanted it.

Marilla looked at her across the table and said nothing, which was Marilla's way of saying a great deal. Davy, who had been told twice to eat his porridge, ate it in a manner meant to show that he was being very good and would like the fact noticed.

The road to the school ran under the beeches and past the pond, and every step of it was as familiar to Anne as her own hand. Yet this morning it looked new, as if somebody had taken it away in the night and put down a stranger road in its place. She found herself rehearsing the speech she meant to make, and found too that she could not remember the first word of it.

There were forty children waiting in the yard. Anne counted them without meaning to, as a person counts the stairs in a dark house, and the number seemed to her enormous.

"Good morning," she said, and her voice sounded to her like somebody else's. She thought, for no reason at all, of Mr. HarrisonMr. Harrison and his parrot, and very nearly laughed.

The laugh did her good. She took the roll from her desk and read the names aloud, one after another, and each child stood when called. Some stood too fast and some too slow. One small boy at the back did not stand at all, but only looked at her with an expression of great and solemn interest, as if she were a new kind of bird.

By ten o'clock she had settled the primer class with their slates. By eleven she had found that Anthony Pye did not intend to be settled by anybody. By noon she understood that a schoolroom had a weather of its own, and that the weather of this one was changeable.

She ate her lunch under the maple by the gate, where she could see the whole yard at once. Diana had wrapped a slice of cake in a napkin and slipped it into her basket that morning, with a note in her round hand that said only "for courage." Anne ate the cake and kept the note.

The afternoon was quieter. A wasp came in at the open window and went out again. The clock on the wall, which had been wrong for eleven years, struck three when it was half past two, and nobody minded. When the last child had gone down the road, Anne sat at her desk in the empty room and listened to the stillness, and found that she was not tired at all, but only very full, the way a cup is full.

Library

  • One layout per size, no list/grid toggle. Phone: 3 covers per row. Wider: as many as fit.
  • Continue card for the book in progress. Desktop shows two.
  • Filters: All, Reading, Finished. Sort is one key.
  • Ribbon = in progress. Green stamp = finished.
  • Series is a slipcase with a count tab. Tap it.
  • Tap a cover to open the book. Long-press or right-click opens the book sheet.
  • Never-opened books (Middlemarch, Meditations, Monte Cristo, volumes 3 and 4) swing their cover open the first time. Others zoom.
  • Come back from Anne of Avonlea to see the Continue bar and page edge grow.
  • Book sheet: Finished thumps a stamp on. Delete makes the cover fall and the shelf close up.
  • Storage line moves to Settings.

Reader

  • Tap the page to show or hide the bars.
  • Four keys: Contents, Summary, Look, More. Search lives in Contents.
  • Phone: panels are bottom sheets. Tablet: they slide over the page. Desktop: docked, the page moves left.
  • One panel at a time. Never stacked.
  • Dashed = planned control, not built yet. It shows where future controls go.
  • Selection tools (highlight, look up, ask, share) come later in the text-selection menu.
  • Search lives in Contents for every book. A result closes the panel, marks the match, and swaps the top bar for a find bar.
  • Original pages: paper sheets on a desk. A page badge shows while the bars are hidden. More holds Reader view.
  • Tap the left or right third to turn a page. It slides 120ms, and nudges at the ends. The middle shows the bars.
  • Look changes the page live: size, font, warmth and line width. Drag the faders.

Summary

  • Series summary folded in as "Before this book".
  • One job, one Stop. Discard becomes Start over in ⋯ with a confirm.
  • The empty state shows what will be summarised instead of explaining it.
  • No provider? Summarise opens setup in place, with the consent text once. Then the summary runs.
  • Story or document switch moves into ⋯. Its tab names follow it, as today.
  • Before this book starts open in the first chapters and folds later.
  • Characters: only names up to your position, so no spoilers. A dot marks a name you added that the next update will use.
  • Editing a character replaces the list inside the panel, with Back. No sheet on top of a sheet.

Motion

  • The library and panels are alive. The reading page is still unless you touch it.
  • Idle motion is slow (6 to 7s), small (2deg) and out of step: ribbons sway.
  • Every press answers: keys sink in 60ms and spring back, stamps thump, covers lift on hover.
  • Transitions show where things come from: books grow from their cover, volumes slide out of the slipcase, panels rise with a small spring.
  • Reduced motion: no idle, no travel, 150ms fades.

First run

  • Empty library: an open book on a ledge. One page turns once on arrival. The ribbon sways. No tour.
  • Dragging files over an empty library lifts a page and lights the ledge.
  • In the first book, the bars start shown and hide on the first page turn. That teaches the tap without any text.
  • Adding: one strip with Cancel. Each book gets an empty slot that fills, then its cover drops in.
  • Desktop and tablet: drag files anywhere on the window.

Series editor

  • Opened with Edit on an open series, or from a book's Series row.
  • The preview is the real slipcase, so a colour or cover change shows at once.
  • Colours are cloth samples. The colour becomes the slipcase tab.
  • Suggested books come from series grouping. + adds one in its place.
  • Nothing is stored until Save, as today.
